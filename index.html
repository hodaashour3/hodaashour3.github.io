PROJECT 01 / 03DATA EXTRACTION

Inbox-to-Spreadsheet
Data Entry

THE PROBLEM

Information arrives by email or form and gets retyped by hand, which is slow and error-prone.

THE SOLUTION

An automated workflow watches the inbox, uses an LLM to extract fields such as name, date, amount, and category, validates the data, and writes clean rows to Google Sheets. Unclear items are flagged for human review.
